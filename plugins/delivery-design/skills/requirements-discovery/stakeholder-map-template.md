# Stakeholders — [Product or Initiative]

> **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

Identify people by **role**, not by name. Roles survive staff changes, stay accurate longer, and are safe to commit to a repository that might one day be shared.

**Influence** means their ability to shape or stop this, not their seniority. A single compliance reviewer often outranks a department head here.

---

## By quadrant

Sorting the table above by interest and influence tells you how to treat each group.

**High influence, high interest — involve.** Decisions need them in the room.

- 

**High influence, low interest — keep satisfied.** They will not engage until something they care about is threatened. Find out now what that is.

- 

**Low influence, high interest — keep informed.** Usually the people who actually use the thing. Their absence from decisions is the most common cause of a built thing going unused.

- 

**Low influence, low interest — monitor.** Note them and move on.
