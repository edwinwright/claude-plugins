## Workarounds

The spreadsheets, the shared inbox, the copy-paste step, the person who "just knows".

These are the most valuable finding in any discovery. A workaround is a requirement someone has already implemented by hand, at their own cost. It tells you precisely where the real constraint is and what an acceptable answer looks like — and a solution that removes the workaround without meeting the need behind it will be rejected.
